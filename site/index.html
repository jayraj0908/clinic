Honest limits

What it will not do.

We read this list out loud with every client before go-live. It makes for a shorter sales pitch and a much longer relationship.

01
It does not send anything on its own.Emails, texts, public posts, price changes, refunds. Every one of those waits for you, and that is enforced in the code rather than asked for in a prompt.
02
It will not invent an answer.If it has not been taught something it says so and takes a message. We would rather it sound limited than sound confident and wrong.
03
It says it is an AI.Whenever asked, and wherever the law requires. No pretending to be a person named Jessica.
04
No medical, legal or financial advice.It books, it answers logistics, it takes messages. Regulated advice goes to the human licensed to give it.
05
Outbound follows the rules, not the funnel.Quiet hours, do not call honoured instantly and permanently, consent required per lead. If that costs us dials, it costs us dials.
06
When it fails, a human phone rings.Fallback forwarding is set up and tested before go-live. Every client, no exceptions.
