SMS Terms & Conditions

Shine Dental Clinic Appointment Notifications · Last updated: July 27, 2026

1. Program description

Shine Dental Clinic's SMS program sends appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and scheduling-related notifications to patients who have booked or requested an appointment and provided their mobile number by phone or through our appointment forms.

2. Consent

By providing your mobile number when booking or requesting an appointment, you consent to receive the appointment-related text messages described above. Consent is not a condition of receiving dental care.

3. Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your appointment activity — typically 1–3 messages per appointment. Message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Opting out

Reply STOP to any message to cancel. You will receive a single confirmation of your opt-out, after which no further messages will be sent. You can re-subscribe by providing your number again when booking.

5. Help

Reply HELP to any message, or call (804) 593-0050 for assistance.

6. Privacy

Your mobile number is never shared with third parties for marketing. See our SMS Privacy Policy.

7. Changes

We may update these terms from time to time; the current version will always be available at this page.
