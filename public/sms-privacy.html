SMS Privacy Policy

Shine Dental Clinic · Last updated: July 27, 2026

This policy describes how Shine Dental Clinic ("we," "us") handles your mobile phone number and text-messaging data for our appointment notification program.

How you opt in (SMS Program Call to Action)

You are only enrolled in this SMS program by giving your mobile number directly to us for appointment purposes, one of two ways:

1. Verbally, during a booking call. When you book or request an appointment on our phone line, you are asked: "What's the best mobile number for your confirmation text? We'll also send a reminder before your visit — message and data rates may apply, and you can reply STOP at any time to opt out." Providing your number in response to that question is your consent to receive appointment-related texts.

2. In writing, on our appointment request forms, which disclose next to the phone-number field that you will receive appointment-related text messages, that message and data rates may apply, and that you may reply STOP to opt out.

We never obtain numbers from third parties, purchased lists, or any source other than you directly. Only people who have booked or requested an appointment receive messages.

What we collect and why

When you book or request an appointment by phone or through our forms, we collect your name and mobile phone number in order to send you appointment confirmations, appointment reminders, and scheduling-related notifications by SMS. These messages are transactional; we do not send marketing messages through this program.

No sharing of your mobile number

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your mobile phone number or your SMS opt-in consent with any third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. Your number is used solely to deliver the appointment-related messages described above, via our messaging service providers acting on our behalf.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with your appointment activity. Typically you will receive 1–3 messages per appointment (a confirmation and up to two reminders). Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan.

Opting out and help

You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (804) 593-0050. After you reply STOP, we will send one final message confirming your opt-out and no further messages will be sent.

Data retention and security

Appointment and contact records are retained as required for clinic operations and applicable healthcare record-keeping requirements, and are protected with industry-standard safeguards.
