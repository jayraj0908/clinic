# Sailz — Client Operations

Every client moves through the same pipeline. One brief per client lives in
this folder — it is the single source of truth for scope, status, and setup.

## Lifecycle (every client, no exceptions)

1. **Discovery call** — their problems in their words. Fill the brief's
   "Problem" section verbatim from this call.
2. **Brief + proposal** — scope, price, timeline, and the honest-limits
   section. Client signs off on WHAT before we build HOW.
3. **Provisioning** — instance folder, env, phone/integrations checklist
   (each brief has its own checklist).
4. **Onboarding** — wizard link to the client; their knowledge in, brain
   born.
5. **Live + first-week watch** — daily check of calls/orders/leads; fix
   fumbles via memory approvals.
6. **Steady state** — weekly report, monthly review call, librarian
   learning loop running.

## Rules

- No client goes live without: signed proposal, owner login handed over,
  emergency fallback tested (what happens when the AI fails), and the
  honest-limits section reviewed WITH the client.
- Every client gets their own instance, own logins, own phone config.
  Nothing shared, ever.
- Scope creep goes in the brief's "Later" section, not into this week.
