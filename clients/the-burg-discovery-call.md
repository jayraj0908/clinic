# The Burg — Discovery Call Script (10–15 min)

Goal: everything needed to build their brain, in their words. Record it
(ask permission) — the brain-dump step can eat the transcript later.

1. **The pain, verbatim** (fills the brief): "Walk me through what happens
   when the phone rings during Friday rush right now." How many calls
   missed? What's an average order worth?
2. **Menu**: get the REAL menu — photo, PDF, or POS export. Every item,
   price, size, and the modifiers people actually say. Ask: "what are the
   5 weirdest special requests you get?" (those become guardrail tests).
3. **Prep times**: normal vs. rush. When is rush?
4. **Kitchen ticket**: where should orders land — text to which phone?
   Email? Printer already on SkyTab? (If SkyTab printer: note for phase 2.)
5. **Hours + holidays**. What should the line say when closed?
6. **Boundaries**: delivery? (v1 pickup-only — confirm they're fine.)
   Phone payments? (No — pay at pickup. Confirm.) Catering/large orders —
   take message for owner?
7. **The voice**: "If your best counter person answered every call, how do
   they sound?" Fast/friendly/short? Any phrases they always say?
8. **Numbers for the close**: current monthly phone orders (rough), what a
   missed dinner-rush call costs. (Sets up ROI in the proposal.)
9. **Shift4 account**: who's their rep? (For the partner API application.)
10. **Set expectations**: week one will fumble some modifier combos — the
    brain learns from every miss, you review what it learned. Demo in ~1
    week: "you'll call the number and order a pizza yourself."
