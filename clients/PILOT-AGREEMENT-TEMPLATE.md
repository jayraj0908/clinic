**PILOT AGREEMENT & LETTER OF INTENT**

Between **[Client business name]** ("Client"), [address],
and **Sailz** ("Provider"), operated by [your name].

**1. The pilot.** Provider will set up and operate an AI phone/automation
service for Client ("the Service"), as demonstrated on [demo date]. The
pilot runs **free of charge** for **[30] days** starting [start date].

**2. What the Service does.** Answers inbound calls on a dedicated line,
takes [orders/bookings], sends kitchen tickets/confirmations, and logs
everything in a dashboard Client can access. The AI identifies itself as
an AI assistant when asked. Client's staff keep full control: call
forwarding can be turned off by Client at any time.

**3. Letter of intent.** If Client is satisfied at the end of the pilot,
Client intends to subscribe at **$[397]/month** (plus a one-time setup
fee of $[0 — waived for pilot clients]), starting [conversion date].
This intent is not a binding purchase; billing begins only when Client
confirms in writing and Provider issues its first invoice. Either side
may walk away at pilot's end, no strings.

**4. Client's part.** Provide the current menu/services and prices, keep
them updated, designate a phone/email for kitchen tickets or alerts, and
set up call forwarding when ready to go live.

**5. Data.** Call recordings, transcripts, orders, and customer contact
data belong to Client. Provider stores them securely to operate the
Service and deletes them on request if the pilot doesn't convert.
Provider may reference Client's name and aggregate results ("answered X
calls, took Y orders") as a case study **only with Client's written OK**.

**6. Honesty clause.** The Service is new. During the pilot it may
occasionally mishandle a complex request; it reads every order back for
confirmation, and when it fails it collects the caller's name and number
for a human callback instead of guessing. Provider reviews recordings
and improves the Service throughout the pilot.

**7. No liability sandbags.** The pilot is provided as-is and free;
neither party owes the other anything if it ends. Nothing in this pilot
transfers ownership of either party's software, brand, recipes, or data
other than as stated in section 5.
